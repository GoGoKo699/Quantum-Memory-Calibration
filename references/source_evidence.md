# Primary-source reading record

Comparison checked on 9 November 2026. A full text being available does not mean that every result in it is used. The locators below identify the parts supporting [related_work.md](related_work.md). Bibliographic fields without verified metadata have been omitted from [references.bib](../references.bib).

| Source | Primary text and inspected material | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Layden, Chen, Cappellaro (2020) | [arXiv manuscript](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1903.01046): common-fluctuator model, supplied-coupling construction, recovery axes, and main-text miscalibration discussion | Supplemental numerical robustness study not inspected |
| Bacon, Lidar, Whaley (1999) | [arXiv manuscript](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/9902041): perturbative stability proof, including the discarded second-order terms | All orders in time must not be read as all orders in perturbation strength |
| Bartlett, Rudolph, Spekkens (2003) | [Primary manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0302111): collective group action and asymptotic coding rates | Comparison concerns a known representation with an unknown common frame |
| Buscemi, Chiribella, D'Ariano (2005) | [Primary manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0504195): random-unitary correction discussion and Eq. (14), entropy exchange versus classical correction-label entropy | Environment-assisted reversal is not a static-profile acquisition theorem |
| Bjelaković, Boche, Nötzel (2008) | [Primary manuscript](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0808.1007): Sec. II averaged-channel definition and Corollary IV.7 | Finite block mixtures are explicitly included |
| Bjelaković, Boche, Nötzel (2008/2009) | [Version 3 manuscript](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0811.4588): channel/code definitions and Theorem 1 | Bibliography uses verified arXiv metadata; no unverified journal fields |
| Buscemi, Datta (2010) | [Primary manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/0902.0158): one-shot entanglement transmission and general correlated-channel sequence capacity | General framework, not an evaluation of the present model |
| Warsi, Coon (2017) | [Oxford accepted manuscript](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid%3Aebd50e8e-d58a-4c51-965f-227fcd6bc397/files/mfad877becf8fd7aa25486ed1fc764f89): Definition 3 and Theorem 3 | General sequences, classical message, side information at encoder |
| Etxezarreta Martinez et al. (2022) | [Primary manuscript](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.13701): Sec. 4.2, quantum outage discussion | Used for context, not as a proof of a general strong converse |
| Danageozian (2023) | [Journal full text](https://quantum-journal.org/papers/q-2023-12-04-1195/pdf/): recovery bounds, spectator-estimation assumptions, and metrological theorem | Acquisition/recovery resource differs from a static description budget |
| Morales-Hashemi, Smith, Cao (2026) | [Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03545): Secs. II–V, fixed data experiment, decision formulation, recovery separation, calibration cost | Encoding and data experiment are fixed, despite arbitrary final recovery |
| Deng (2026) | [Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30910): nine-qubit Gaussian model, preparation choices, calibration and execution budget | Fixed code, instrument, recovery, and control menu |
| Ilin, Pereg (2026) | [Primary abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23459): relay access to occurred Pauli errors, compression, and matching capacity claims | Full primary text unavailable during this comparison; exact formulas not used |
| Quantum-Spectral-Memory | [Pinned theorem source](https://github.com/GoGoKo699/Quantum-Spectral-Memory/blob/ee5dc0e5529c7775bfaeabb2a5e744c51eb9431d/science/spectral_law.md): supplied-spectrum occupancy and Schur comparison | Methods and calibrated baseline are inherited |
| Inhomogeneous-Collective-Memory | [Pinned model source](https://github.com/GoGoKo699/Inhomogeneous-Collective-Memory/blob/b6bbf3cae33b300d0c793628e168616dc4919bdc/science/model.md): supplied coupling profile and allowed endpoint operations | Known-profile construction informs the achievable baseline |

The relay-paper limitation is substantive: its abstract supports the stated distinction in side-information access, but it does not support a claim that every theorem in that paper has been ruled out as a route to a related information bound. The comparison does not use secondary summaries of its formulas.
